## Key facts from arXiv 2310.15833 (context only)

- It tested 691 on-target and 176 off-target TOIs.
- It gave the right on/off-target call in 96.6% of cases.
- It ranked the true host first in 38 of 40 known off-target cases.
- Its authors say the method ranks hosts and does not statistically confirm them.
- It reports no definitive crowding result.

These numbers are context. We do not claim to beat them. Our setting (injected transits in real pixels, our own splits, a shortlist instead of a rank) is not directly comparable.
